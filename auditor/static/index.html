Did the market really cause this increase?

A supplier says the material went up because of imports and freight. Paste the last invoice and today’s quote. This page shows how much of that increase a cited price series explains, and a reply you can send back.

How to try it

  1. Step 1Keep the sample invoice, or paste your own last invoice on the left.
  2. Step 2Keep the sample quote, or paste today’s quote or WhatsApp message on the right.
  3. Step 3Press “Check the explanation.” Read the three numbers, then the reply.

How far the reasons can be trusted

The arithmetic is exact for the prices on the page. The market reasons are only as good as the price series behind them. This demo uses a labeled sample series, not today’s mill price. Freight and currency count only when the supplier mentions them and the series has a value on both dates. A different grade or thickness stops the check. This does not forecast next month’s steel price.
